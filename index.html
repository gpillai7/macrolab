Core Design Philosophy

What makes MacroLab different

01 —

Interactions, not isolated signals

Most intelligence platforms track macro signals independently. MacroLab maps how they interact — and watches for the moments when those interactions amplify into non-linear pressures.

02 —

Thresholds matter more than levels

A signal at 6 is not simply worse than a signal at 4. MacroLab monitors the boundaries where gradual pressure becomes structural shift — and flags when systems approach those thresholds.

03 —

Narratives over point estimates

Precise forecasts create false confidence. MacroLab generates structured narratives — coherent interpretations of how macro forces are evolving and what they mean for business decisions.

04 —

Singapore policy mediates everything

Singapore's industrial, FX, labour, and fiscal policy levers fundamentally alter how global shocks transmit into the domestic economy. MacroLab keeps the policy layer central.

05 —

Labour as a structural regime variable

06 —

China as a political-economy OS

Singapore Policy Lens

How Singapore Policy Mediates Macro Transmission

Singapore's policy architecture is not passive. FX management, labour frameworks, industrial strategy, and fiscal buffers actively reshape how global shocks transmit into the domestic economy.

FX Policy

MAS Exchange Rate Framework

SGD NEER management provides structural inflation buffering. MacroLab tracks band positioning, slope adjustments, and their downstream effects on import cost transmission.

Labour Policy

Fair Consideration & Manpower Frameworks

COMPASS, PMET policies, and EP/S-Pass quota shifts interact with global AI displacement to reshape Singapore's talent cost structure across sectors.

Industrial Policy

EDB, ITM, and Sector Roadmaps

Industry Transformation Maps and EDB investment mandates define which sectors receive structural policy support — shaping resilience differentials across the economy.

Fiscal Buffering

Reserves, GST Vouchers & Support Schemes

Singapore's deep fiscal reserves enable counter-cyclical interventions. MacroLab tracks when fiscal space is deployed and how it reshapes the business operating environment.

Scenario: US-China Tariff Escalation to 145%+

A further escalation in US-China tariffs beyond current levels would activate three simultaneous transmission pathways into Singapore's economy. First, trade flow disruption: Singapore's role as an ASEAN trade hub creates direct exposure as Chinese exporters accelerate ASEAN rerouting, creating both opportunity (higher port throughput) and risk (secondary tariff scrutiny). Second, manufacturing margin compression: Singapore's precision engineering and electronics manufacturing sectors face accelerating upstream cost pressure as Chinese component suppliers adjust pricing structures. Third, demand-side deceleration in Singapore's financial services sector as regional corporate investment decisions are deferred pending trade policy clarity.
